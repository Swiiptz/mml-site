# The EPITA adventure

I started my journey in computing back in primary school, booting Linux distributions on the family’s Asus EeePCs—nothing beats Linux for experimenting. That thread continued in middle school with my first bits of code, first on Scratch and then in Python. In high school, I dove into the deeper side of things through the NSI track, discovering algorithms, SQL, and more.

Today, at EPITA, I’m strengthening my foundations in advanced algorithms, software architecture, mathematics, and programming, while continuing to build—on my free time—prototypes of apps, tools, and sometimes small video games. I love discovering new technologies and learning continuously.

# What drives me

I’m motivated by the direct impact of what I build on users, and by constantly discovering new technologies. My curiosity pushes me to explore, understand, and experiment. Solving a complex bug, optimizing a system, or seeing a feature I built tangibly improve a user’s experience is a real source of satisfaction for me.
